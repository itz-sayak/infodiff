\section{EPT-TPP: a neural point process with an exact compensator}\label{sec:ept}

Neural marked TPPs (RMTPP, NHP, THP, SAHP, AttNHP, S2P2) either restrict the intensity to
a simple parametric form between events or estimate the compensator
$\int\lambda$ by Monte Carlo. We keep the phase-type algebra of Section~\ref{sec:theory}
and let a recurrent network drive it.

\paragraph{State and dynamics.}
The latent state $\bm x(t)\in\R_{\ge0}^P$ consists of $C$ channels of Erlang-$R$ chains with
learnable rates $\beta_{ck}$. Between events it evolves as
$\dot{\bm x}=\bm F_\beta\bm x$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:recursion}). At event $n$ with mark
$m_n$ and gap $\Delta_n$, a GRU summary $\bm h_n$ produces a gate and a nonnegative
jump,
\[
\bm x(t_n^+)=\sigma(W_g\bm h_n)\odot\bm x(t_n^-)+\mathrm{softplus}(W_j\bm h_n).
\]

\paragraph{Intensity.}
For marks $m=1,\dots,M$,
\[
\lambda_m(t)=\mu_m(\bm h_n)+\sum_p C_{mp}\,x_p(t)+g_m(\bm h_n)\,e^{w(\bm h_n)(t-t_n)},\qquad
C,\mu,g\ge0,
\]
where the last (Gompertz) term allows increasing as well as decreasing hazards.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:ept}
(i) $\lambda_m>0$ everywhere. (ii) The compensator between events is exact:
$\int_{t_n}^{t_n+\Delta}\lambda_m=\mu_m\Delta+\bm C_m^\top\bm I(\Delta)+g_m\,\mathrm{expm1}(w\Delta)/w$
with $\bm I(\Delta)$ given by Lemma~\ref{lem:recursion}. (iii) With $g=0$, identity gates and
jumps $\mathrm{softplus}(W_j\bm h_n)=\bm e_{m_n}\otimes\bm\beta$ the model is the
multivariate phase-type Hawkes process of Section~\ref{sec:model}; with $C=0$ it is
RMTPP~\citep{du2016recurrent}.
\end{proposition}

Consequently training maximises the \emph{exact} log-likelihood (no sampling variance
in gradients), evaluation reports the exact test likelihood, and next-event
predictions are Bayes-optimal functionals of the exact density:
$\E[\Delta]=\int_0^\infty S(s)\dd s$ and
$\hat m=\arg\max_m\int_0^\infty\lambda_m(s)S(s)\dd s$, computed by one-dimensional
quadrature.
