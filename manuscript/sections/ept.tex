\section{EPT-TPP: a neural point process with an exact compensator}\label{sec:ept}

Neural marked TPPs (RMTPP, NHP, THP, SAHP, AttNHP, S2P2) either restrict the intensity to
a simple parametric form between events or estimate the compensator
$\int\lambda$ by Monte Carlo. We keep the phase-type algebra of Section~\ref{sec:theory}
and let a recurrent network drive it.

\paragraph{State and dynamics.}
The latent state $\bm x(t)\in\R_{\ge0}^P$ consists of $C$ channels of Erlang-$R$ chains with
learnable rates $\beta_{ck}$. Between events it evolves as
$\dot{\bm x}=\bm F_\beta\bm x$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:recursion}). At event $n$ with mark
$m_n$ and gap $\Delta_n$, a GRU summary $\bm h_n$ produces a gate and a nonnegative
jump,
\[
\bm x(t_n^+)=\sigma(W_g\bm h_n)\odot\bm x(t_n^-)+\mathrm{softplus}(W_j\bm h_n).
\]

\paragraph{Intensity.}
For marks $m=1,\dots,M$,
\[
\lambda_m(t)=\mu_m(\bm h_n)+\sum_p C_{mp}\,x_p(t)+g_{1m}(\bm h_n)\,e^{-a(\bm h_n)s}
+g_{2m}(\bm h_n)\bigl(1-e^{-\kappa(\bm h_n)s}\bigr),\qquad s=t-t_n,
\]
with $C,\mu,g_1,g_2,a,\kappa\ge0$: bounded decaying and rising-but-saturating hazards (an
unbounded Gompertz term overflows on heavy-tailed gaps).

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:ept}
(i) $\lambda_m>0$ everywhere. (ii) The compensator between events is exact:
$\int_{t_n}^{t_n+\Delta}\lambda_m=\mu_m\Delta+\bm C_m^\top\bm I(\Delta)
+g_{1m}(1-e^{-a\Delta})/a+g_{2m}\bigl(\Delta-(1-e^{-\kappa\Delta})/\kappa\bigr)$
with $\bm I(\Delta)$ given by Lemma~\ref{lem:recursion}. (iii) With $g=0$, identity gates and
jumps $\mathrm{softplus}(W_j\bm h_n)=\bm e_{m_n}\otimes\bm\beta$ the model is the
multivariate phase-type Hawkes process of Section~\ref{sec:model}; with $C=0$ it is
RMTPP~\citep{du2016recurrent}.
\end{proposition}

Consequently training maximises the \emph{exact} log-likelihood (no sampling variance
in gradients), evaluation reports the exact test likelihood, and next-event
predictions are Bayes-optimal functionals of the exact density:
$\E[\Delta]=\int_0^\infty S(s)\dd s$ and
$\hat m=\arg\max_m\int_0^\infty\lambda_m(s)S(s)\dd s$, computed by one-dimensional
quadrature.

\subsection{EPT-X: a renewal channel for within-cascade timing}\label{sec:eptx}
Order-book streams contain cascades: a single marketable order produces several messages
with the same timestamp, and inter-event gaps span ten orders of magnitude, from
nanoseconds to seconds. A linear Hawkes intensity describes excitation \emph{between}
cascades well but has no direct handle on the gap distribution \emph{within} them. EPT-X adds, by
superposition, a second channel that models the \emph{next gap} directly while keeping the
compensator exact.

\paragraph{Defective hyper-Erlang channel.} Fix a dictionary of $J$ Erlang atoms
$(R_j,\beta_j)$ whose means $R_j/\beta_j$ lie on a logarithmic grid spanning the data's
time scales, with orders $R_j\in\{1,4,16\}$ (coefficient of variation $R_j^{-1/2}$, so
high orders give sharp modes in log-time). After event $n$ the history $\bm h_n$ selects
weights $\bm w(\bm h_n)$ on the simplex over the $J$ atoms and a \emph{defect} atom $w_\infty$,
and a mark law $q_{m\mid j}(\bm h_n)$ for each time scale. The channel's joint density of the
next gap $s$ and mark $m$ and its survival function are
\[
p(s,m)=\sum_{j=1}^J w_j\,q_{m\mid j}\,\frac{\beta_j^{R_j}s^{R_j-1}e^{-\beta_js}}{(R_j-1)!},\qquad
S_X(s)=w_\infty+\sum_{j=1}^J w_j\,e^{-\beta_js}\sum_{i<R_j}\frac{(\beta_js)^i}{i!}.
\]
EPT-X superposes this channel on EPT (including its Hawkes backbone):
$\lambda_m(t)=\lambda^{\mathrm{EPT}}_m(t)+p(s,m)/S_X(s)$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:eptx}
(i) The compensator over $(t_n,t_n+\Delta]$ is $\Lambda^{\mathrm{EPT}}(\Delta)-\log S_X(\Delta)$, in closed
form. (ii) With $w_\infty=1$ EPT-X is exactly EPT, which in turn nests the multivariate
phase-type Hawkes process. (iii) Given $\bm h_n$, the renewal channel is the absorption
time of a finite continuous-time Markov chain (a phase-type law), so its survival,
quantiles and moments are closed-form matrix functionals. (iv) Since mixtures of
Erlang distributions are dense in the distributions on $(0,\infty)$
\citep{tijms1994stochastic,lee2010modeling}, the channel can approximate any conditional
gap law as the dictionary is refined---the expressiveness of a conditional mixture
density \citep{shchur2020intensity}, with an exact intensity and compensator.
\end{proposition}

The two channels have distinct roles: the renewal channel describes \emph{within-cascade}
timing and the mark mix of a cascade (the mark law depends on which time scale fires),
while the Hawkes channel describes \emph{cross-cascade} excitation. The GRU also receives
the standardised log-gap, because a linear gap feature cannot distinguish nanosecond ties
from zero.
