\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Making every kernel a nonnegative combination of phase-type densities turns Hawkes
estimation into a certified convex problem and turns news responses into matrix
exponentials. Three findings stand out. First, the ``half-life'' of news is not a
property of the news kernel: echoes lengthen absorption by one to two orders of
magnitude, and the correction is available in closed form. Second, falsification
matters: our first two specifications produced plausible-looking news effects that the
placebo test exposed as artefacts of long kernels, ties and boundary bias. Third, a
linear Hawkes backbone makes a neural point process both exact and competitive on
order-book data. Limitations: expectations for payrolls, claims, PPI and retail sales
are statistical rather than survey-based; the FX feed has a gap in the first half of
2023; neural baselines were trained with reduced batch sizes on an 8\,GB GPU; and on the
public EasyTPP benchmarks EPT does not reach the state of the art (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
