\section{Data}\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Releases.}
The calendar covers January 2022--August 2026 (670 scheduled events). US data releases
(CPI, nonfarm payrolls, PPI, retail sales, GDP advance estimate, initial jobless claims)
are dated by their \emph{initial} vintage in ALFRED, which removes seasonal-factor-only
releases automatically; all are released at 08:30 America/New\_York and converted to UTC
with daylight saving. FOMC statements (14:00 ET) and press conferences (14:30 ET) come
from the Federal Reserve calendar; ECB decisions and press conferences from the ECB
(13:45/14:30 CET until 21 July 2022 and 14:15/14:45 CET afterwards); Bank of Japan
decisions from the BoJ, whose release time is not fixed (a latent onset, flagged).

\paragraph{Expectations and surprises.}
CPI expectations are the Cleveland Fed inflation nowcast for the reference month, taken
from the last nowcast strictly before the release date; GDP expectations are the Atlanta
Fed GDPNow forecast immediately before BEA's advance estimate. For payrolls, claims, PPI
and retail sales we use real-time statistical expectations from the sequence of first
prints (mean of the previous 3, 1, 6 and 6 prints respectively); survey consensus is not
freely available and this limitation is stated wherever surprises are used. Surprises
are standardised by the expanding standard deviation of past surprises of the same
type (no look-ahead) and winsorised at $\pm4$.

\paragraph{Prices.}
FX (EUR/USD, USD/JPY), gold (XAU/USD) and an S\&P~500 CFD come from HistData
millisecond bid/ask ticks (stamped in fixed EST, converted to UTC); BTC and ETH from
Binance USD\textsuperscript{\tiny M}-futures aggregated trades. A clock check at a
CPI release confirms activity jumps within one second of the scheduled time in both
sources. The HistData feed has a gap from February to July 2023 for all four
instruments; windows without at least 90\% of 10-minute blocks containing five ticks
are excluded (per window, documented in the data card).

\paragraph{Events and windows.}
Prices are converted to $\delta$-crossing events with $\delta$ calibrated per asset so
that the median intraday rate is about 0.2 events per second:
$\delta=0.35$ (EUR/USD), $0.25$ (USD/JPY), $0.7$ (XAU/USD), $0.7$ (S\&P~500 CFD) and
$3.0$ (BTC, ETH) basis points. Releases closer than 60 minutes are merged into one
window $[\text{first}-30\,\text{min},\ \text{last}+60\,\text{min})$ with 30 minutes of
burn-in history. Each window is matched with a placebo window at the same UTC clock
times on the same weekday one to three weeks away and with no scheduled release within
three hours; its pseudo-releases carry the PLACEBO type.
