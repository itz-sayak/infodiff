\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Track A: synthetic recovery}
Table~\ref{tab:track-a} reports the held-out likelihood deficit (means and standard
deviations over five seeds); branching-matrix, spectral-radius and kernel errors are in
Table~\ref{tab:track-a-full} (Appendix).
When the truth is a single exponential (S1) the correctly specified single-decay
estimators (tick's exponential MLE and ADM4) are best, as they must be. Under a true
power law (S2) the sum-of-exponentials estimators---tick's and MSX---are statistically
tied (held-out deficit $0.2$ vs.\ $0.3\times10^{-3}$ nats/event; tick's solver failed on
one of five seeds). Whenever the kernel has structure outside a single family---a
delayed hump (S3), components ten milliseconds and thirty seconds apart (S4), or a sparse
ten-dimensional power-law network (S5)---MSX-auto is best on every metric: its held-out
deficit is 7--10 times smaller than the best competitor's, its branching-matrix error
is roughly halved, and it recovers the causal graph perfectly (AUC $=1$). The
exponential-only ablation isolates the contribution of Erlang phases, and the
certificate of Proposition~\ref{prop:gap} was at most $3.3\times10^{-4}$ nats over all 100 MSX fits.

\subsection{Main study: how fast do markets absorb scheduled macro news?}
The pooled 2022--2026 model (\nWindows{} windows---\nNewsWindows{} release and
\nPlaceboWindows{} placebo---with \nEventsScored{} million scored $\delta$-crossing events,
\nEventsAll{} million including burn-in history, in twelve dimensions) is certified optimal to within \gapMaxPooled{} nats per dimension. The
spectral radius of the branching matrix is $\rho(G)=\rhoPooled$: roughly seven in every
eight price events are endogenous echoes. Fitted year by year, $\rho(G)$ declines from 2022
to 2026 (\rhoByYear), and the slowest echo mode relaxes in about \relaxPooled{} minutes.

\paragraph{Falsification.} Figure~\ref{fig:placebo} compares, for every release type, the
direct news-kernel mass with that of a matched placebo at the same clock time on a day
without any scheduled release (neither the \nKinds{} modelled types nor sixteen second-tier
US releases). With the exposure-weighted $\ell_1$ level selected on held-out windows,
\nPassPlacebo{} of the \nKinds{} release types exceed their placebo by at least 50\%: CPI
(\placeboCpi$\times$), payrolls (\placeboNfp$\times$), PPI (\placeboPpi$\times$), the FOMC
statement (\placeboFomc$\times$) and retail sales (\placeboRetail$\times$). Jobless claims,
ECB and BoJ decisions and the press conferences do not have a detectable \emph{direct}
effect beyond their placebos---an honest negative that the unregularised fit would have
hidden: without the penalty, nonnegative news kernels absorb as much mass on placebo days
as on release days (boundary bias), and the first specification with long news kernels
failed the test outright (Appendix).

\paragraph{Echo-corrected absorption.} For the releases that pass the placebo test, the
direct news kernel is absorbed almost instantly (median $t_{50}$ of \tDirMed{}\,s), but the
market's own echoes stretch the response: half of the total extra activity arrives within
\tEchoLo--\tEchoHi{}\,s and ninety percent within \tNinetyLo--\tNinetyHi{} minutes
(Figure~\ref{fig:absorption}, Table~\ref{tab:absorption}). The textbook ``news
half-life'' $\ln 2/\beta$ therefore understates absorption time by one to two orders of
magnitude.

\paragraph{Price discovery.} Signed surprises enter the news kernel separately by sign, so
the model yields expected drift curves (Figure~\ref{fig:drift}). The signs are economically
coherent: hotter-than-expected inflation and stronger payrolls depress crypto and lift the
dollar, while cooler prints do the opposite.

\paragraph{Goodness of fit.} Time-rescaled residuals have Kolmogorov--Smirnov statistics of
at most \ksMax{} against Exp(1), and the per-window excess-dispersion test rejects in
\edLo--\edHi\% of windows, compared with 42\% (power-law kernel) and 93\% (double
exponential) reported for EBS FX data by \citet{rambaldi2015modeling}.

\subsection{Track B: public marked-TPP benchmarks}
Table~\ref{tab:track-b} compares our models with the published numbers of
\citet{chang2025deep} under the same protocol; our re-run of S2P2 on Taxi (0.524 against
the published 0.522) validates the harness. On Retweet, the largest dataset (2.2 million training
events), EPT-X improves on the best published model by more than 0.6 nats per event, with
a 25-epoch budget imposed by compute and the validation likelihood still rising. It is
statistically tied with the best published model on Taobao and StackOverflow, and close on
Taxi. There our time term equals
S2P2's (0.733 vs.\ 0.735 nats per event on the re-run) and the whole difference lies in the
mark term. On Amazon, whose inter-event times concentrate in a narrow band (median
0.73, 99th percentile 0.80), the Erlang orders used elsewhere (coefficient of variation
$\ge 1/4$) cannot represent the mode; adding atoms of order up to 1024 (coefficient of
variation $1/32$), evaluated through the regularised incomplete gamma function, raises
EPT-X by 1.1 nats per event on both validation and test, into second place behind S2P2.
Neither a deeper (three residual layers) nor a wider (128 units) encoder improved the
validation likelihood on any dataset. The renewal channel adds 0.07 nats per event over
EPT on Taxi and 0.02 on StackOverflow. The linear MSX model is close to the neural models
on Taobao (1.305) and Retweet, and far behind on Amazon and Taxi, where the dynamics are
not self-exciting.

\subsection{Track C: limit-order-book event streams}
Table~\ref{tab:track-c} reports test log-likelihood per event on the five LOBSTER
stocks. EPT-X is the best model on every stock, ahead of MSX-auto, EPT and all seven
neural baselines; among the stocks on which every baseline has finished, our models win
\trackCwins{} of \trackCdone{}, by \trackCmarginLo--\trackCmarginHi{} nats per event over the
best neural baseline. One baseline needs a correction. EasyTPP's IntensityFree clamps
every inter-event time at $10^{-5}$\,s before evaluating its log-normal-mixture density,
so all shorter gaps are scored as one common value on which the mixture can place an
arbitrarily narrow component. This is not the likelihood of the observed gaps, and it
matters most where short gaps are common: 29\% and 32\% of test gaps on INTC and MSFT lie
below $10^{-5}$\,s, against 10--14\% on the other three stocks. As shipped, IntensityFree
therefore reports the highest numbers on INTC and MSFT (row marked $\ast$); re-trained and
scored on the unclamped gaps (clamp $10^{-12}$\,s, same 1500-epoch budget) it falls by
1.1 and 0.7 nats per event, below EPT-X and on INTC below MSX. The patch is part of the
replication package, and the as-shipped numbers are excluded from the best-model marks.
Consistently, a denser MSX dictionary (40 instead of 12 rates) and sharper,
history-shifted Erlang atoms in EPT-X change the validation likelihood on INTC by at most
about 0.05 nats per event, an order of magnitude less than the clamp: the shipped margin
came from the clamp, not from expressiveness.
Among the remaining neural
baselines, recurrent and
attention models with Monte-Carlo compensators (NHP, THP, AttNHP) fall well behind the
exact-likelihood phase-type models. Two implementation notes matter for fairness. First,
LOBSTER timestamps contain ties, which EasyTPP rejects; all models are trained and
scored on the same files in which tied messages are displaced by $U(0,100\,\text{ns})$.
Second, on our 8\,GB GPU the neural baselines run with batch sizes 32--64 instead of 256.
Every neural model is trained with a 100-epoch budget and the strongest competitors
additionally with longer budgets; for each model the table reports the budget with the
best \emph{validation} likelihood, so longer training can only help the baselines. The
Hawkes backbone is essential for EPT: without it EPT underperforms plain MSX (superseded
run, Appendix). The renewal channel of EPT-X adds \eptxGainLo--\eptxGainHi{} nats per event over EPT on
every stock.

number of $\delta$-crossings in the first 1, 5 and 15 minutes (Table~\ref{tab:track-d}).

target; we report this negative result as is. For the \emph{direction} of the post-release
move the structural model is informative: the correlation between the forecast and the
