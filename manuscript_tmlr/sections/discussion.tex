\section{Discussion and limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

Writing every kernel as a nonnegative combination of phase-type densities makes Hawkes estimation
a convex problem whose solution can be certified, and it turns the response to a shock into a
matrix exponential. Adding a neural history encoder and a mixture-of-Erlang renewal channel to the
same state space gives a model whose likelihood is still exact, which trains with a parallel scan
and which can start from the certified linear fit. This model is competitive with the strongest
neural temporal point processes, and better than them on the order books, Retweet and Taobao.

We draw three conclusions. First, the speed at which news is absorbed is not a property of the
news kernel alone. The market's own echoes lengthen it by one to two orders of magnitude, and the
correction can be computed in closed form. Second, falsification tests are worth building in. Two
of our earlier specifications produced plausible news effects that the placebo test showed to be
artefacts. Third, benchmark comparisons should check that every model is scored on the same
observed data and that the data carry the structure the benchmark is meant to test. A single clamp
inside one implementation was enough to reverse a ranking, and the Amazon gaps are drawn from two
fixed intervals.

\paragraph{Limitations.} For payrolls, claims, PPI and retail sales our expectations are
statistical forecasts rather than survey consensus, and the FX data have a gap in the first half
of 2023. The order-book evaluation uses the public LOBSTER samples, which cover one trading day per
stock. On the order books, the neural baselines were trained with smaller batches than their
authors used, to fit an 8\,GB GPU. On the public benchmarks we compare with published numbers and
have checked our evaluation with one re-run. On Retweet, EPT-X was trained for 25 epochs, and the
certified start could not be used there without stalling training. EPT-X does not reach the best
published likelihood on Taxi, where the remaining gap is in the time term, or on Amazon. Its mark
accuracy trails the best published models on most datasets. Finally, the MSX forecasts of
post-release activity are beaten by regressions fitted directly to that target.
